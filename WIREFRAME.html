Login card: opacity + y:−24, .4s.

Hero collage: rotate −5↔5, mirror, 8s infinite + per-tile hover scale 1.04.

Badge counters (cart/wishlist): AnimatePresence scale 0→1 pop on change.
AddToCart qty bubble: scale 0→1 pop + animate-ping ring; stock progress bar width 500ms; in-cart panel fade + y −10.
🧱 Framer Motion — dropdowns / drawers / overlays▶

Spinners: animate-spin (login submit, apply discount "...").
⏱️ Live timers & reactive motion▶
Hero sale countdown: updates every 30s, SSR-safe "--d --h --m" placeholder.
Product flash-sale countdown: HH:MM:SS ticking every second.
Search dropdown: auto-close 7s timer, pauses on hover.
Testimonials: instant page swap (no slide transition — candidate for improvement).
⚠️ Motion inconsistencies worth flagging▶
• Homepage "Womens/Footwear" headings use text-5xl font-extrabold vs newer sections' text-3xl/4xl font-bold.
• /products card is a different design (fixed width, object-contain) vs homepage card (aspect 4/5, quick-add, zoom) — two product-card languages coexist.
• Add-to-cart gradient (blue→purple) clashes with the black/white storefront language.
• Testimonials have no slide/carousel transition (instant swap).
• No page-level route transitions (only per-section entrances).
• Dark mode is partially applied — several storefront sections have no dark: variants (e.g. /products cards, wishlist).
